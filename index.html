What does pool maintenance cost per month?

Monthly maintenance plans typically range from $100-$225/month depending on pool size, visit frequency, and chemical needs. We’ll customize a plan that fits your pool and schedule.

How long does a new pool take to build?

Most inground pool builds take 8-14 weeks from permit approval to finish, depending on design complexity, weather, and material lead times. We’ll give you a realistic timeline upfront.

How do I know if I need a new liner?

Signs include visible fading, cracks, wrinkles, or unexplained water loss. Most vinyl liners last 10-15 years. We offer free liner inspections to assess your situation honestly.

How much does liner replacement cost?

Liner replacement cost varies depending on pool size, liner pattern/thickness, and any wall or floor repairs needed. We provide a free, itemized quote before any work begins.

What thickness liner should I get?

We offer liners with different thickness. Thicker liners are more durable and puncture-resistant. We’ll recommend the right thickness based on your pool usage and budget.

How long will a new liner last?

A properly maintained vinyl liner typically lasts 10–15 years. Proper water chemistry, avoiding sharp objects, and winterizing correctly all extend liner life significantly.

Do I need to do anything to prepare?

Just clear the pool deck area so our crew can work safely. We handle everything else – draining, old liner removal, disposal, and installation. You just need to be available to refill with water after.

Are you licensed and insured in Oklahoma?

Absolutely. Redbud Pool Co. is fully licensed and insured in the state of Oklahoma. We pull all required permits and follow all local building codes on every project.
