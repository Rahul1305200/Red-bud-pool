Pool maintenance

Clear water. Every week. No stress.

Pool chemistry is more complex than most people expect. Off-balance water damages your liner, corrodes equipment, and makes swimming uncomfortable – or unsafe. Our trained technicians keep everything dialed in so you never have to think about it.

Every visit: what our technicians do

Water Chemistry

We test for chlorine, pH, alkalinity, calcium hardness, and CYA - then add exactly what’s needed to bring everything into range.

Skimming & Brushing

We skim the surface, brush the walls and floor, and empty all skimmer and pump baskets - preventing algae before it starts.

Vacuuming

Manual or automatic vacuuming of the pool floor to remove debris, dirt, and any early-stage algae before it becomes a problem.

Equipment Check

We inspect your pump, filter, heater, and automation system and flag any issues early - before a small problem becomes an expensive repair.

Weekly visits or occasional seasonal help. We service pools of all types and sizes in Bartlesville and surrounding areas, and we have a plan that fits. Monthly plans typically range from $100–$225/month depending on pool size, visit frequency, and chemical needs.

Vinyl liner replacement

A fresh liner transforms your pool.

A fresh liner transforms your pool completely - better look, better seal, better swimming. If your pool liner is fading, cracking, or leaking – replacement is the most cost-effective way to restore your pool to like-new condition. Redbud Pool Co. measures, orders, and installs your new liner with precision and care.

The liner replacement process

  1. 1

    Free Inspection

    We measure your pool precisely and assess the current liner condition, walls, and floor for any repairs needed before installation.

  2. 2

    Choose Your Liner

    We bring samples and walk you through pattern, color, and thickness options to find the perfect match for your pool and style.

  3. 3

    Installation Day

    We drain your pool, remove the old liner, make any wall or floor repairs, and install your new liner.

  4. 4

    Fill & Balance

    We start filling your pool and provide a startup chemistry kit so your water is balanced and swim-ready as quickly as possible.

What does pool maintenance cost per month?

Monthly maintenance plans typically range from $100-$225/month depending on pool size, visit frequency, and chemical needs. We’ll customize a plan that fits your pool and schedule.

How long does a new pool take to build?

Most inground pool builds take 8-14 weeks from permit approval to finish, depending on design complexity, weather, and material lead times. We’ll give you a realistic timeline upfront.

How do I know if I need a new liner?

Signs include visible fading, cracks, wrinkles, or unexplained water loss. Most vinyl liners last 10-15 years. We offer free liner inspections to assess your situation honestly.

How much does liner replacement cost?

Liner replacement cost varies depending on pool size, liner pattern/thickness, and any wall or floor repairs needed. We provide a free, itemized quote before any work begins.

What thickness liner should I get?

We offer liners with different thickness. Thicker liners are more durable and puncture-resistant. We’ll recommend the right thickness based on your pool usage and budget.

How long will a new liner last?

A properly maintained vinyl liner typically lasts 10–15 years. Proper water chemistry, avoiding sharp objects, and winterizing correctly all extend liner life significantly.

Do I need to do anything to prepare?

Just clear the pool deck area so our crew can work safely. We handle everything else – draining, old liner removal, disposal, and installation. You just need to be available to refill with water after.
